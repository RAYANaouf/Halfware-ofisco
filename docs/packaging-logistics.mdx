  <Tab title="Custom Field">
    ## Item

    | Field | Label | Type |
    |---|---|---|
    | `custom_colissage` | Colissage | Check |

    ## Line Item Doctypes

    Applies to **Purchase Order Item**, **Purchase Receipt Item**, and **Delivery Note Item**.

    | Field | Label | Type | Depends On |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `custom_colissage` | Colissage | Check | `item_code.custom_colissage` |
    | `custom_qte_colissage` | Qte Colissage | Float | `eval:doc.custom_colissage` |
    | `custom_nbr_coli` | Nbr Coli | Float | `eval:doc.custom_colissage` |

    <Note>
      `custom_qte_colissage` and `custom_nbr_coli` are only visible and mandatory when `custom_colissage` is enabled.
    </Note>

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Field details">
        <ParamField body="custom_colissage" type="Check">
          Indicates whether the item uses colissage. Fetched from `item_code.custom_colissage`.
        </ParamField>
        <ParamField body="custom_qte_colissage" type="Float">
          Quantity per colis. Shown only when `custom_colissage` is enabled.
        </ParamField>
        <ParamField body="custom_nbr_coli" type="Float">
          Number of coli. Visible and mandatory when `custom_colissage` is enabled.
        </ParamField>
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
